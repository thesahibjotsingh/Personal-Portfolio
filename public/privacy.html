Privacy

What I collect.

Only what you choose to send me, and a little anonymous information so the site can show the right prices. No ads, no tracking cookies, nothing sold.

What you send

  • The brief. Your name and email, your answers about the project, and a WhatsApp number and notes if you add them. It is delivered to my inbox through EmailJS, an email-sending service, and I use it only to reply to you and to plan your project.
  • WhatsApp or email. If you message me directly, those services' own rules apply to the message itself.

What the site works out on its own

  • Your country, not your location. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, tells it which country a visit comes from so prices can be shown in rupees, US dollars or Canadian dollars. It is used on the spot and not stored.
  • Your currency choice. If you pick a currency, your browser remembers it on your own device. It is never sent to me.
  • Anonymous visit counts. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits, pages and countries. It sets no cookies and builds no profile of you.

Who sees it

Me, and the two services that carry it: EmailJS (delivering the brief to my inbox) and Cloudflare (hosting and the visit counts). I do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long I keep it

Enquiries stay in my inbox as long as I need them to reply and keep a record of the conversation. Ask me to delete yours sooner and I will.

Your choices
